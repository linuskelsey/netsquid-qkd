# bonizzoni-2020-npjQI

Bonizzoni et al. (Univ. Modena, 2020), published in npj Quantum Information 6:68, demonstrated storage and retrieval of microwave pulses using a molecular spin ensemble quantum memory. Oxovanadium tetraphenyl porphyrin (VO(TPP)) diluted crystals were embedded in a planar NbN superconducting resonator and measured at 2 K and B0=0.24 T. The bare Hahn echo phase memory time was Tm=0.97±0.10 µs. Applying Carr-Purcell (CP) and Uhrig dynamical decoupling sequences with N=3–4 π-pulses extended the effective memory time up to approximately 3 µs, a factor of three enhancement. Trains of up to five weak microwave pulses were stored in a single crystal sample and retrieved with the time ordering of the pulse sequence reversed upon retrieval, as expected from the echo mechanism. Phase information of the stored pulses was faithfully reproduced in the retrieved echoes. The work demonstrated that molecular spin systems, which are synthetically tunable and processable, constitute a viable platform for time-domain multimode microwave quantum memories, with the chief advantage that molecular spin properties can be engineered through chemical design to optimise coupling and coherence for specific quantum memory architectures.
