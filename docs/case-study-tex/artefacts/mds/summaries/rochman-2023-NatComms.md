# rochman-2023-NatComms

Rochman, Xie et al. (Caltech/Faraon group), published in Nature Communications 14:1153 (2023), demonstrated microwave-to-optical transduction using erbium ions simultaneously coupled to planar photonic and superconducting resonators. Er3+:YVO4 (560 ppm) provided the spin ensemble; an amorphous Si photonic resonator was integrated with an Nb superconducting resonator on the crystal surface. The microwave ensemble coupling was g_µ,tot/2π=105 MHz (ground state at 60 mT), yielding microwave cooperativity C_µ=340. The optical ensemble coupling was g_o,tot/2π=2.0 GHz with optical cooperativity C_o=4.1. The mode overlap between microwave and optical modes was F=9.5×10^-4. CW transduction efficiency was η_d=2–3×10^-9. Using pulsed excitation (1 µs optical pulses with τ_off=100 ms) at 5 mW optical power, the excited-state transduction efficiency reached η_d=10^-7, with spin temperature below 100 mK and microwave resonator heating below 0.15 quanta at the 100 ms off-time. While the absolute transduction efficiency remains low, the work validated the dual-resonator Er platform as a coherent microwave-to-optical transducer with high cooperativity on both channels, establishing a blueprint for bidirectional quantum state conversion between superconducting qubit and optical fiber networks.
