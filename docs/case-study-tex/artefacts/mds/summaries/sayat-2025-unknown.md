# sayat-2025-unknown (Sayat et al. 2025 — A*STAR Singapore)

Sayat, Lee, Negi, Iwahara, Seo, Tan, Lam, Cho and Soh (A*STAR/CQT Singapore), preprint July 2025. The paper characterises the optical properties of Er³⁺:CaWO4 — an emerging rare-earth-ion-doped crystal candidate for quantum memories — focusing on the polarisation and temperature dependence of the Z1→Y1 (1532.6 nm) and Z1→Y2 (1530.8 nm) optical transitions in a 50 ppm Er³⁺-doped CaWO4 crystal at temperatures down to ~3 K. Two crystal orientations were studied (k∥c and k∥a). The Z1→Y1 transition with k∥c is identified as the most favourable configuration for quantum memory: it exhibits polarisation independence, a stable central wavelength, narrower linewidth (implying longer lifetime and storage time), larger absorption cross-section, and sits within the C-band telecommunications window at 1532.6 nm. Absorption decreases with increasing temperature for both transitions and both axes. Crystal cell parameters are stable below ~10 K, setting the ideal operating temperature. The paper addresses a knowledge gap — prior Er³⁺:CaWO4 work focused on the microwave regime (coherence times of 1.3 ms and 23 ms achieved), while optical regime characterisation was lacking. The CaWO4 host is attractive due to its low nuclear spin bath (most nuclei have no nuclear spin), in contrast to Y2SiO5 where the only stable yttrium isotope is nuclear-active.
