# uysal-2024-unknown

Uysal, Dusanowski, Xu, Horvath, Ourari et al. (Princeton/Thompson group, 2024) demonstrated spin-photon entanglement between a single Er3+ ion and telecom-band photons. Er3+:CaWO4 was coupled to a Si nanophotonic cavity with Purcell factor P=342 and radiative lifetime τ_r=18.4 µs for the B optical transition, at T=500 mK. A modified time-bin entanglement protocol was used in which XY-16 dynamical decoupling was applied simultaneously to both the ground and excited spin manifolds during the photon emission window, mitigating optically-induced spin dephasing from charge noise. The measured entanglement fidelity was F=0.73(3), with per-axis entanglement witnesses {E_X, E_Y, E_Z}={0.60(3), 0.55(3), 0.77(5)}. The entanglement generation rate over 15.6 km of optical fiber was 1.48 Hz at λ=1532.6 nm. Optically-induced dephasing was characterised: σω ∝ |sin(2φ-2φ0)|, confirming electric-field (charge noise) origin consistent with the S4 site symmetry of Er3+ in CaWO4. Spin T2 without optical pulses: 30 µs (Hahn echo) and >200 µs (XY-16). This established Er3+:CaWO4 in nanophotonic cavities as a viable spin-photon entanglement source for quantum repeaters operating at telecom wavelengths.
