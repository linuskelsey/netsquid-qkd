# eisenach-2021-NatComms

Eisenach, Barry et al. (MIT Lincoln Laboratory), published in Nature Communications 12:1357 (2021), demonstrated cavity-enhanced microwave readout of a solid-state spin ensemble. An NV-centre ensemble in diamond (≈1.4×10^15 polarised spins) was coupled to a composite microwave cavity with intrinsic quality factor Q0=22,000. The collective coupling strength extracted from the avoided crossing was 2g_eff=2π×1.4 MHz, yielding cooperativity ξ=1.8. Cavity-based readout produced a contrast C=0.97 compared to C=0.05 for conventional optically detected magnetic resonance (ODMR), representing a factor of ~20 improvement. Cavity-mediated line narrowing reduced the readout linewidth from 8.5 MHz (ODMR) to 4 MHz (MW cavity), improving spectral resolution. Magnetometry performance approached the Johnson-Nyquist noise limit when probing a test field at 10 Hz. The work showed that crossing the cooperativity threshold ξ>1 with NV centres enables near-unity-contrast microwave spin readout without optical detection, circumventing the low photon-collection efficiency that limits ODMR sensitivity. This approach establishes cavity-enhanced microwave readout as a practical alternative for NV-based sensors and as a model for spin-ensemble quantum memory readout where high-fidelity state detection is required.
