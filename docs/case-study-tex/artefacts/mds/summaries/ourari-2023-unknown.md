# ourari-2023-unknown

Ourari, Dusanowski, Horvath, Uysal et al. (Princeton/Thompson group, 2023) demonstrated indistinguishable photon emission from a single erbium ion in the solid state at telecom wavelengths, a key requirement for quantum network applications. A single Er3+ ion was implanted at 10 nm depth in CaWO4 and coupled evanescently to a Si photonic crystal nanobeam cavity at T=0.47 K in a 3He cryostat. PLE spectroscopy revealed a single-scan optical linewidth of 150 kHz and long-term spectral diffusion of only 63 kHz r.m.s. over 12 hours. The Purcell factor was P=850, reducing the radiative lifetime from 6.3 ms to τ=7.4 µs. Hong-Ou-Mandel interference using photons with a 36 km fiber delay yielded raw visibility 80(4)% (corrected: V_int=84%), confirming photon indistinguishability. Spin coherence measurements gave T1=3.7 s, T2*=247 ns (Ramsey), T2=44 µs (Hahn echo), and T2>200 µs with XY64 dynamical decoupling. This was the first demonstration of indistinguishable photons from a single solid-state ion at telecom wavelengths, with the spin coherence properties sufficient to enable spin-photon entanglement, directly relevant to quantum repeater nodes based on rare-earth ions in nanophotonic cavities.
