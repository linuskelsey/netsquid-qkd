# appel-2024-unknown

Appel et al. (Cambridge, Gangloff and Atatüre groups, 2024) demonstrated a many-body quantum register using the nuclear spin ensemble of a GaAs droplet quantum dot, targeting its use as a quantum memory for a central electron spin qubit. Approximately 13,000 71Ga nuclear spins were prepared into a collective dark state with spin length j≈0.6·j0 via optical pumping at 4 K and 4.5 T. Write and read operations used 130 ns SWAP gates driven by the NOVEL sequence at Hartmann-Hahn resonance, transferring coherence between the electron qubit and a collective nuclear magnon mode. A full write-store-retrieve-readout protocol achieved 68.6(4)% raw fidelity with a storage time of 130(16) µs without dynamical decoupling. Magnon Ramsey interferometry resolved a Knight field frequency split of 0.500(12) MHz, directly confirming coherent magnon oscillations in the register. Nuclear dephasing T2,n* =130(16) µs was found consistent with quadrupolar broadening limits. The work established that semiconductor quantum dot nuclear spin ensembles can serve as coherent, addressable quantum registers with sub-microsecond gate times, offering a scalable pathway for on-chip quantum memory integrated with photonic quantum dot platforms.
