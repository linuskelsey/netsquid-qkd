# oehrl-2025-unknown

Oehrl, Fesquet et al. (Walther-Meißner-Institut Munich, 2025) probed a phosphorus donor spin ensemble in silicon using squeezed microwave signals generated by a Josephson parametric amplifier (JPA). Phosphorus donors in isotopically purified 28Si ([P]=1×10^17 cm^-3, 18 µm thick) were coupled to a Nb lumped-element resonator via flip-chip assembly, achieving cooperativity C=0.3. The JPA produced squeezing up to 5.3 dB below the vacuum noise level. Wigner tomography was used to reconstruct the quantum state of the output microwave field at various magnetic field detunings. With only the resonator in resonance, noise increased by S_resonator=+1.47±0.03 dB relative to the squeezed reference (S_off=-5.29±0.09 dB). When spins were additionally brought on resonance, noise increased further to S_spins=+0.12±0.03 dB above vacuum, with a transfer efficiency |t(0)|²≈61% extracted from the model. Numerical optimisation demonstrated that transfer efficiency approaches unity for C~1 conditions. The work established that spin-ensemble microwave memories can be characterised and operated with non-classical squeezed input states, relevant to quantum networks where squeezed microwave states are used as resources, and confirmed that C≥1 is the key threshold for efficient quantum state transfer into spin memories.
