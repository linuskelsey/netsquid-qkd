# franco-rivera-2022-unknown

Franco-Rivera, Cochran et al. (Florida State University/NHMFL, 2022) demonstrated strong coupling between a Gd3+ multilevel spin system and an on-chip superconducting resonator. Gadolinium (S=7/2, zero orbital angular momentum) at 0.05% concentration in a CaWO4 single crystal was coupled to a Nb coplanar stripline resonator at T≈0.38 K. A large avoided crossing of 146 MHz was observed in transmission spectroscopy. Exact diagonalisation of the full S=7/2 multilevel system yielded a collective coupling gc/(2π)=57.35 MHz and cooperativity C≈35 (C≈58 under a two-level approximation). The average single-spin coupling was g0≈620 Hz. Notably, the fourth-order crystal-field parameter B4_4 was significantly perturbed (~130 MHz shift) in the presence of the photon field, indicating a perturbation of the crystal ground state by the cavity, a unique feature of the high-spin multilevel system. Pulsed ESR revealed Rabi-like population inversion dynamics consistent with active spin cooling via the cavity. The work established Gd3+ in CaWO4 as a strongly coupled multilevel spin system, with the high spin multiplicity offering richer internal structure for encoding quantum information and potentially enabling more complex spin-cavity interactions than two-level spin systems.
