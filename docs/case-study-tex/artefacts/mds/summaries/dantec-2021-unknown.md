# dantec-2021-unknown

Le Dantec, Rančić et al. (CEA Paris-Saclay, Bertet group), published in Science Advances 7, eabj9786 (2021), demonstrated a Hahn echo electron spin coherence time T2=23.2±0.5 ms for Er3+ ions in a natural-abundance CaWO4 crystal. Er3+ was present at an extremely low concentration of 0.7±0.1 ppb, and three superconducting Nb microresonators operating at 7–8 GHz were patterned directly on the crystal surface for measurements at 10 mK. The coherence decay followed a stretched exponential with exponent x=2.4±0.1 at the optimal field orientation φ=47°, where the effective g-factor is minimised. Decoherence was attributed primarily to the 183W nuclear spin bath (14% natural abundance, I=1/2, γn=1.8 MHz/T); cluster correlation expansion (CCE) simulations predicted T2,n=27.2 ms from this bath alone, placing the measured value near the nuclear-spin-limited ceiling. ESEEM oscillations from 183W were directly observed. Spin-lattice relaxation T1 entered the Purcell-enhanced regime at the lowest temperatures. This was the longest electron spin T2 measured in a natural-abundance material without operating at a clock transition, demonstrating that CaWO4 is an exceptionally spin-quiet host and a prime candidate for rare-earth-based microwave quantum memories.
