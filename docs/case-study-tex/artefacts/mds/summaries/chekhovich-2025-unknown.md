# chekhovich-2025-unknown

Dyte, Manna, Covre da Silva, Rastelli and Chekhovich (2025 preprint, later published as dyte-2025-NatComms) demonstrated over 100 ms of quantum coherence storage in the nuclear spin ensemble of a single GaAs/AlGaAs quantum dot. The system contained approximately 5×10^4 nuclei (75As, 69Ga, 71Ga, all spin-3/2); uniaxial elastic strain isolated the Iz=±1/2 central transition (CT) of 75As, with a CT linewidth of ~0.8 kHz versus satellite linewidth ~13.8 kHz (first-order quadrupolar splitting νQ(1)≈255.1 kHz at Bz≈5.16 T). FID coherence time T2*≈0.46 ms and Hahn echo T2^HE≈1.38 ms were measured. Applying the CHASE-40 supercycle dynamical decoupling sequence (40 π-pulses per cycle) with 48 repeated cycles extended T2 to 106.6 ms; the maximum spin memory time was TM≈136 ms at a cycle time TCycle≈2 ms. Residual decoherence was attributed to effective three-body nuclear spin interactions not suppressed by pairwise decoupling. Coherence extension was shown to be independent of the initial spin state phase, confirming uniform decoupling. This result set a record storage time for a semiconductor quantum dot nuclear spin ensemble and demonstrated the viability of quantum dot nuclear registers as long-lived quantum memories.
