# greggio-2026-unknown

Greggio, Lorriaux, Petrescu, Mirrahimi and Bienfait (ENS Paris/Lyon, 2026) developed a quantum cascaded model for optimal absorption and emission of itinerant microwave fields into a spin ensemble memory. The theory derives the time-dependent cavity linewidth modulation κ(t) required to maximise storage and retrieval efficiency for arbitrary input pulse shapes. The fundamental upper bound on efficiency is η ≤ κs/(κs+κ0), where κs=4g_ens²/Γ is the spin-induced decay rate and κ0 is the intrinsic cavity loss rate, showing that internal losses set a hard ceiling independent of pulse shaping. In the continuous-drive limit, the effective cooperativity satisfies C*≤1, bounding achievable efficiency. A critical pulse bandwidth α* exists above which absorption efficiency drops sharply, setting a minimum pulse duration of approximately 2/Γ for near-optimal operation. Numerical simulations used spin parameters from two leading experimental platforms: Bi donors in 28Si-enriched silicon (T2^Bi≈1 s) and 171Yb3+:CaWO4 (T2=0.15 s). The work provides a complete theoretical framework connecting spin coherence parameters, cavity geometry, and pulse bandwidth to the ultimate efficiency limits of spin-ensemble microwave quantum memories, directly guiding the engineering of memory protocols for superconducting qubit quantum networks.
